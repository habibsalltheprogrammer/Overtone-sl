Inspiring a Generation of Leaders

Empowering Young People. Inspiring Tomorrow’s Leaders.

Overtone Sierra Leone is committed to inspiring and equipping young people through mentorship, leadership development, creative expression, and opportunities that help them discover their potential.

Discover Our MissionGet Involved

Young people at the centre

Overtone Sierra Leone is a youth-focused organisation established around 2018. We believe young people grow into confident, responsible, value-driven leaders when they are mentored, trained and given room to create.

Our work centres on youth empowerment, mentorship, skill-building and creative community engagement across Sierra Leone.

Meet the founder

Driven by a passion for youth empowerment and leadership, Khadison Duwai founded Overtone to help inspire, mentor, and equip a generation of young people to discover their potential and create meaningful impact.
