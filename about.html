About Overtone Sierra Leone

Inspiring a Generation of Leaders.

Who we are

Overtone Sierra Leone (Overtone SL) is an organisation that focuses on youth empowerment, mentorship, skill-building, value-driven leadership and creative community engagement in Sierra Leone.

Our story

Overtone SL was established around 2018 by Khadison Duwai, out of a passion for helping young people discover their potential.

Our mission

To empower young people through mentorship, leadership development, creative expression, and practical opportunities that help them become confident, responsible, and value-driven leaders.

Our vision

To inspire a generation of leaders equipped to influence their communities positively and contribute to meaningful social change.

Our core values

  • IntegrityDoing what is right, consistently.
  • ServiceUsing leadership to lift others.
  • GrowthLearning and improving every day.
  • CreativityGiving talent room to express itself.
  • CommunityBuilding together, for everyone.

Our approach to youth empowerment

We see young people as capable and full of potential. Our approach combines mentorship, leadership development, practical skills and creative expression, so growth happens in character as well as ability.

Our commitment to community development

Leaders are measured by how they serve. We encourage young people to use what they learn to strengthen their families, neighbourhoods and communities.

Be part of what comes next

Volunteer, mentor, partner or simply join the community. Tell us how you would like to take part.
