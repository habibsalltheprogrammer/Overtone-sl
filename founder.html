Our Founder

Khadison Duwai, Founder of Overtone Sierra Leone.

Khadison Duwai

Founder, Overtone Sierra Leone

Khadison Duwai founded Overtone Sierra Leone around 2018, driven by a passion for youth empowerment and leadership.

His aim is to help inspire, mentor, and equip a generation of young people to discover their potential and create meaningful impact.

Why Overtone began

Overtone grew from a commitment to young people: that they deserve mentorship, encouragement and real opportunities to grow.

Commitment to mentorship, empowerment and leadership

Through Overtone SL, Khadison works to see young people grow into confident, responsible and value-driven leaders.

Leadership philosophy

Leaders are shaped by values, mentorship and opportunity.
